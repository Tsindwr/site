## GM-less Play

If you do not want to play under the classical "1 GM and many players" format, this would be one option available to you! Playing without a GM and sharing the narrative responsibilities can allow the group to feel more like they are sharing a collaborative experience. This means there is no GM stat sheet, and any Tests initiated by a player's action would, instead of being chosen by a GM, be chosen by a second player, who tells the first player which Test to roll to complete their action. 

A similar practice applies to would-be GM actions; creature or environment actions that would be resolved by a stat block or the GM's stats, would instead be resolved by a player, as long as their character was not the sole receiver of the action's consequences, by stating if the action has a "positive" or "negative" outcome. Based on the player's decision, gameplay continues to the best of the table's ability to continue the story now that the action's outcome has been determined. For example, if a player were to decide that their character is going to attempt navigating a storm on boat, then another player would volunteer, and for the purposes of this example, states that the outcome is "negative." The player whose character is navigating the storm would now describe how the scene turns for the worse, in whichever manner they deem fitting for the story they are telling.

Some situations may require improvisation from the GM to establish the details of the world around the characters. In GM-less mode, this gives players an opportunity to chime in and establish the scene, or the features and behavior of a non-player character, or a twist in the story as something unexpected occurs. Any player taking up the role of an improvising GM in this way is eligible for a Flavor Token after the conclusion of their description or contribution.
