(Utility, +1 String)

    • On each turn, you must make a melee attack against another creature. If there are none within range, you must spend your Movement Action to move towards the nearest creature, and then take your Attack Action. If there are no adversaries within range, this ability targets your allies. Allies within Here range may use a reaction to spend 1 Flavor Token to narrate how they calm your rage.     Avatar of the Cosmos

    Cost: 2.5 Strings (1 Enhancement)

    Prerequisite: Stardust Pool/Humanoid’s Best Friend (-2 Strings)

    Short Rest Action (2 Strings)

    Direct Focus: 

    Once per Long Rest, you may to attune to one of the many Constellations in the cosmos. In order to attune to a Constellation, you must learn a facet of its True Name; this involves conducting a ceremony requiring ingredients made from rare living residents (whether flora or fauna) of the Domain corresponding to the god related to the Constellation (Material Components, -1 String). This ritual must be conducted under the divine light of the respective god’s moon (Narrow Trigger, -1 String), and, in order to ground the memory of comprehending a True Name, requires the caster to resolve an important social conflict, confess a deep emotion, or other narrative disclosure that connects with the target Domain (Narrative Consequence, -5 Beats).

    Attuning to a Constellation allows you to purchase an Option for the Flipside of this Action*. It also gives you a fleeting understanding of the Constellation’s Essence, allowing you to expend a use of this ability to instead apply the target Domain to a single Test*. When you make a Test in this way, you may improvise a Utility-level magical effect as part of the Test’s intent*. You may choose to let your Familiar activate this Ability in this way instead of you (Agency, +1 String). Using this Ability in this way does not prevent you from using the Flipside, it just uses up a charge for the Focus side*.
